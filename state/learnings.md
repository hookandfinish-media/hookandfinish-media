# Learnings (update every run, keep it short, numbers only from real data)

## Baseline (Nov 5–24, 2026)
- Gumroad: ~13 real visitors in 30 days, 0 sales. Traffic/clicks is the bottleneck.
- Instagram story Reels (history facts, cartoon, narrated, 25–60 s): 118–1,839 views, avg watch 10–18 s. Best: "Monks who stole silk" 1,839 views (Nov 12); "Aran sweater myth" 318; "Irish famine" 232.
- Instagram product/tutorial Reels (Nov 5–8): 7–24 views. Avoid direct product pitches.
- Instagram slideshow tip Reels (Nov 20–23): 11–119 views, avg watch 2.6–3.3 s. Weak first frame; don't use as the default.
- Instagram photo/carousel posts: ~0 reach (account has 2 followers).
- Pinterest early pins (Nov 5–10, linked to the kit): 8–35 impressions each, 3–8 clicks each (20–50% click rate!). New pins from Nov 19 onward: 0 impressions after 1–4 days (Pinterest is slow with new pins).
- TikTok: carousels ~637 views (Mistakes, 2.7/8 slides viewed) vs videos ~200.
